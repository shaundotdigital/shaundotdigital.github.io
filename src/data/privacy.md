## Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to collect statistics about visits: which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are and what kind of device they use. I use this only to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

- It also records how the site is used: which links and buttons are clicked, how far down the page you scroll, which sections you reach and where you leave. Email addresses are never sent.
- Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser (named `_ga` and `_ga_*`) to tell visits apart. They last up to two years.
- Advertising features, Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off.
- Google processes the data on my behalf. I do not use it to identify you.
- Analytics does not load for visitors in the EU, EEA or Switzerland, or if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal.

## No cookie banner

UK law allows analytics used purely for statistics to run without asking for consent first, as long as you are told about it and can easily opt out. This site relies on that rule.

## Opting out

Use the “Opt out of analytics” link in the footer of any page. Your choice is saved in your browser and applies from the next page you load. You can opt back in the same way.
